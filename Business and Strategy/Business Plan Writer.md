# Business Plan Writer
> Transforms vague business ideas into comprehensive, investor-ready business plans with financial projections and market validation.

## Purpose
This enhancer converts simple business concepts into structured, detailed business plans that cover all critical sections: executive summary, market analysis, competitive landscape, value proposition, business model, marketing strategy, operations plan, financial projections, and funding requirements. It ensures completeness, logical flow, and professional formatting suitable for investors, lenders, or internal strategic planning.

## Best For
- Entrepreneurs preparing business plans for funding applications
- Founders seeking to validate and articulate their business concept
- Small business owners writing plans for SBA loans or grants
- Students and teams creating business plan competitions
- Strategic teams documenting new business unit or product launches
- Consultants building plans for clients

## Prompt Enhancer
```text
Act as a seasoned business strategist and financial analyst. Transform the user's business idea into a comprehensive, professional business plan. Use web search to gather current market data, industry benchmarks, and competitor information when needed.

Follow this structure rigorously:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   - Business name, mission statement, and vision (1-2 sentences each)
   - Problem statement with supporting market data
   - Proposed solution and unique value proposition
   - Target market definition with TAM/SAM/SOM sizing (use web search for current market figures)
   - Revenue model and projected unit economics
   - Funding requirement and use of funds
   - Key milestones for next 3 years

2. COMPANY DESCRIPTION
   - Legal structure and ownership
   - Company history or founding story
   - Core values and culture principles
   - Location and facilities
   - Intellectual property or proprietary advantages

3. MARKET ANALYSIS
   - Industry overview with current size, growth rate, and trends (cite sources from web search)
   - Target market segmentation (demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioral)
   - Customer personas (2-3 detailed profiles with pain points, goals, buying behavior)
   - Market validation evidence (interviews, surveys, pilot results, or comparable data)
   - Regulatory environment and compliance considerations

4. COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS
   - Direct, indirect, and potential future competitors
   - Competitor matrix comparing features, pricing, market share, strengths, weaknesses
   - Competitive moat and defensibility strategy
   - Barriers to entry for new competitors
   - SWOT analysis specific to this venture

5. PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
   - Detailed description of offerings
   - Product development roadmap (MVP to full feature set)
   - Pricing strategy with rationale (cost-plus, value-based, competitive)
   - Intellectual property strategy (patents, trade secrets, trademarks)
   - Future product pipeline

6. MARKETING AND SALES STRATEGY
   - Brand positioning statement
   - Marketing channels and budget allocation percentages
   - Customer acquisition cost (CAC) targets and channels
   - Sales process and funnel stages
   - Customer retention and lifetime value (LTV) strategy
   - Partnership and distribution channels
   - Content marketing and thought leadership plan

7. OPERATIONS PLAN
   - Day-to-day operational workflow
   - Supply chain and vendor management
   - Technology infrastructure requirements
   - Quality control processes
   - Key operational metrics and KPIs
   - Scalability plan

8. MANAGEMENT TEAM
   - Organizational chart
   - Key team members and their relevant experience
   - Advisory board or mentors
   - Hiring plan for next 12-24 months
   - Compensation structure overview

9. FINANCIAL PLAN
   - Revenue projections (3-5 year monthly for Year 1, quarterly for Years 2-3, annually for Years 4-5)
   - Cost of goods sold (COGS) and gross margin analysis
   - Operating expenses breakdown
   - Cash flow statement projections
   - Break-even analysis with timeline
   - Funding requirements and use-of-funds breakdown
   - Key financial assumptions and sensitivity analysis

10. APPENDIX
    - Supporting research data
    - Detailed financial model assumptions
    - Team resumes or bios
    - Letters of intent or customer testimonials
    - Product mockups or screenshots if applicable

For each section, provide specific numbers, percentages, and timelines rather than vague statements. Use industry benchmarks from current data. Flag any assumptions that need validation and suggest how to test them. End with a list of 5 critical risks and corresponding mitigation strategies.
```

## Example
### Original Prompt
```text
Write a business plan for a meal kit delivery service.
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
Act as a seasoned business strategist and financial analyst. Transform the user's business idea into a comprehensive, professional business plan for a meal kit delivery service. Use web search to gather current market data on the meal kit industry (projected market size, growth rate CAGR, key competitors like HelloFresh, Blue Apron, Freshly), consumer trends (health consciousness, convenience demand, sustainability preferences), and industry benchmarks for customer acquisition costs, churn rates, and average order values.

Follow this structure rigorously:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   - Business name, mission statement, and vision
   - Problem statement: what specific pain point in meal planning/grocery shopping/healthy eating does this solve? Include data on how many hours consumers spend meal planning weekly and what percentage report difficulty eating healthy.
   - Proposed solution with 3-4 key differentiators vs. HelloFresh and Blue Apron
   - Target market with TAM (US meal kit market), SAM (specific region/dietary niche), SOM (realistic Year 1 penetration)
   - Revenue model: subscription tiers, average order value, frequency, and projected unit economics (contribution margin per box)
   - Funding requirement with specific use-of-funds allocation percentages
   - 3-year milestone timeline with specific metrics

[Continue through all 10 sections with meal-kit-specific detail, citing current market data found via web search. For financials, include specific assumptions: average box price, cost of ingredients, packaging costs, shipping costs, customer acquisition cost by channel, monthly churn rate benchmarks for food subscriptions.]
```

## Notes
- Always use web search to validate market size claims and competitor data rather than relying on potentially outdated training data
- Financial projections should include clearly stated assumptions so readers can adjust them
- The enhancer forces specificity — vague phrases like "growing market" get replaced with actual figures
- Encourage users to supplement with real data from customer interviews, surveys, or pilot programs
- Works best when the user provides at least a basic concept before enhancement
