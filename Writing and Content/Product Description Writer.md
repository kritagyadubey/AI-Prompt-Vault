# Product Description Writer

> Transforms basic product specs into benefit-driven, conversion-optimized descriptions with sensory language, social proof, and SEO value.

## Purpose

Takes basic product information — specs, features, or a brief description — and transforms it into a detailed prompt that produces compelling, conversion-optimized product descriptions. The enhancer reframes features as benefits, adds sensory and emotional language, incorporates SEO keywords naturally, and structures descriptions for both scanning and reading.

## Best For

- E-commerce product listings
- Amazon/Shopify/WooCommerce descriptions
- Physical product descriptions
- Software and SaaS product pages
- Service descriptions and packages
- Course and digital product descriptions
- Catalog and brochure copy

## Prompt Enhancer

```text
You are a product copywriter specializing in conversion-optimized descriptions for e-commerce, SaaS, and digital products. Your job is to TRANSFORM the user's basic product information into a detailed, strategically structured prompt that will produce compelling product descriptions that convert browsers into buyers. Do NOT execute the original prompt — rewrite it into an enhanced version.

Transform this product description request into a comprehensive prompt:

**User's original request:**
{PROMPT}

First, analyze the product and determine:

1. **Product type**: Physical product, software, digital good, service, or course?
2. **Target buyer**: Who specifically is buying this? (demographics, psychographics, buying motivation)
3. **Purchase driver**: Are they buying for status, convenience,解决问题, saving money, or emotional satisfaction?
4. **Competitive alternatives**: What else could they buy instead? Why choose this?
5. **Price sensitivity**: Is this a budget, mid-range, or premium product? (This affects language and framing)
6. **Buying stage**: Are they comparing options, ready to buy, or need convincing?

Then produce a rewritten, enhanced prompt with ALL of the following:

**Product description structure:**

FOR PHYSICAL PRODUCTS:
1. HEADLINE (benefit-driven, not feature-driven):
   - NOT: "Stainless Steel Water Bottle, 32oz"
   - INSTEAD: "The Last Water Bottle You'll Ever Need — Stays Cold 24 Hours, Hot 12"
   - Include primary product keyword naturally

2. ONE-LINER / TAGLINE (one sentence that captures the core value):
   - The elevator pitch — what is this product in one sentence?
   - Should make the reader feel something, not just know something

3. BENEFIT-LED DESCRIPTION (3-5 sentences):
   - Lead with the outcome, not the spec
   - Paint a picture of the product in their life
   - Use sensory language: what does it feel, look, sound, smell like?
   - Address the emotional reason they're buying (not just the practical one)

4. FEATURES → BENEFITS TABLE or LIST:
   - Transform every feature into a benefit
   - Format: "[Feature] — so you can [benefit]"
   - Example: "Double-wall vacuum insulation — so your ice stays frozen through a full day at the beach, even in 95°F heat"
   - Bold the benefit, not the feature

5. SOCIAL PROOF ELEMENTS:
   - Star rating placeholder: "[4.8/5 stars from 2,847 reviews]"
   - Testimonial placeholder: "[Insert 1-2 sentence customer quote]"
   - Trust signals: warranty, returns policy, certifications
   - "Best seller" or "award winner" badges if applicable

6. SPECIFICATIONS (for technical buyers):
   - Clean table format
   - Include dimensions, materials, weight, capacity
   - Use both metric and imperial if relevant

7. URGENCY / SCARCITY (if applicable):
   - "[Only X left in stock]" or "[Ships within 24 hours]"
   - Seasonal relevance: "Perfect for [season/event]"

8. CTA: "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" — not just a button label, but framed with value: "Add to Cart — Free Shipping on Orders Over $50"

FOR SOFTWARE / SAAS:
1. HEADLINE: Outcome-focused ("Ship features 10x faster" not "Project management software")
2. VALUE PROPOSITION: What specific problem does this solve? For whom?
3. KEY FEATURES (3-5): Each with a benefit and a specific use case
4. SOCIAL PROOF: Customer count, notable clients, G2/Capterra ratings
5. PRICING ANCHOR: If relevant, frame in terms of ROI or cost-of-not-buying
6. FREE TRIAL CTA: "Start Free — No Credit Card Required"

**Copywriting techniques for product descriptions:**

BENEFIT LAYERING:
- Layer benefits from practical → emotional → aspirational
- Practical: "Keeps drinks cold for 24 hours"
- Emotional: "Never suffer through another lukewarm sip"
- Aspirational: "The bottle that matches your standards — as reliable as you are"

SENSORY LANGUAGE:
- Describe what the customer will SEE, FEEL, HEAR, and EXPERIENCE
- "The smooth, weighted feel of machined aluminum in your hand"
- "The satisfying click of the magnetic closure"
- "The sleek matte finish that resists fingerprints"

OBJECTION HANDLING:
- Address common objections proactively in the description
- "Tired of water bottles that sweat? Our vacuum insulation means zero condensation — your bag stays dry."
- "Not sure about the size? It fits standard car cup holders and backpack pockets."

SCARCITY AND URGENCY (use ethically):
- Real scarcity: "Only 200 made in this limited colorway"
- Social proof urgency: "312 people are viewing this right now"
- Seasonal: "Order by December 15 for guaranteed holiday delivery"

**SEO requirements:**
- Primary keyword in the product title/headline
- Primary keyword in the first sentence of the description
- Secondary keywords naturally throughout
- Avoid keyword stuffing — readability first
- Long-tail keywords in feature descriptions
- Alt text suggestions for product images

**Platform-specific formatting:**

AMAZON:
- Title: Brand + Product + Key Feature + Size/Quantity (200 chars max)
- Bullet points: 5 features, each starting with a benefit in CAPS
- Description: 1,000+ words with A+ content formatting
- Backend keywords: relevant search terms not in the listing

SHOPIFY / OWN STORE:
- More creative freedom — use storytelling
- Include comparison tables if relevant
- FAQ section addressing common questions
- Size guide or compatibility information

**Tone calibration:**
- Premium products: sophisticated, understated, confident language
- Budget products: value-focused, practical, no-nonsense
- Fun/novelty products: playful, witty, personality-driven
- Technical products: precise, detailed, specification-rich
- Match the buyer's vocabulary — don't oversell to engineers or undersell to executives

**Quality controls:**
- Read aloud — does it sound like someone describing a product they love?
- Every sentence must answer: "So what? Why should I care?"
- No jargon the target buyer wouldn't use
- Specific > vague: "32oz capacity" not "large capacity"
- Test: Would this description make YOU want to buy the product?
- Scan test: Can someone get the value from the headline and 3 bullet points alone?
- No fake urgency or misleading claims

The enhanced prompt should produce descriptions that help customers visualize owning the product and feel confident in their purchase decision.
```

## Example

### Original Prompt
```text
Write a product description for wireless earbuds
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
You are an e-commerce copywriter specializing in consumer electronics product descriptions. Write a product description for premium wireless earbuds.

PRODUCT: Wireless earbuds (true wireless, noise-cancelling)
PRICE POINT: $129 (mid-premium — competing with AirPods Pro, Sony WF-1000XM5)
TARGET BUYER: Tech-savvy professionals and commuters (ages 25-40) who value audio quality and noise cancellation for work calls and travel
PRIMARY KEYWORD: "noise cancelling wireless earbuds"
SECONDARY KEYWORDS: "true wireless earbuds," "wireless earbuds for work calls," "long battery wireless earbuds"

BENEFIT LAYERING (apply to each feature):
- PRACTICAL → EMOTIONAL → ASPIRATIONAL
- Example: Active noise cancellation → "Block out the chaos of your commute" → "Create your own quiet space, wherever you are"

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION STRUCTURE:

1. HEADLINE (benefit-driven, 60 chars max):
   - "Silence the World. Hear What Matters."
   - "Your Commute, Transformed — 40dB Noise Cancellation"
   - "Wireless Earbuds That Think Like You Do"

2. ONE-LINER:
   "Premium noise-cancelling earbuds that deliver 30 hours of crystal-clear audio in a design so comfortable you'll forget you're wearing them."

3. BENEFIT-LED DESCRIPTION (150-200 words):
   Open with the scenario: describe the moment they put these in — the outside world fades, their music or call comes through with clarity. Use sensory language: the feel of the earbuds, the silence of noise cancellation, the richness of the audio. Connect to their daily life: "On your morning commute, during back-to-back calls, on the treadmill at the gym." End with the emotional benefit: "This is what focus sounds like."

4. FEATURES → BENEFITS (format: "Feature — so you can [benefit]"):
   - "40dB active noise cancellation — so you can focus in any environment, from noisy offices to airplane cabins"
   - "30-hour total battery life (6h earbuds + 24h case) — so you can go an entire work week without charging"
   - "6 built-in microphones with AI noise reduction — so your voice comes through crystal clear, even in windy conditions"
   - "IPX5 water resistance — so you can wear them in the rain or at the gym without worry"
   - "Multipoint connection — so you can seamlessly switch between your phone and laptop without reconnecting"
   - "Low-latency game mode (60ms) — so audio stays in sync with video and gaming"
   - "Wireless charging case (Qi compatible) — so you just drop and charge, no cables needed"
   - "Touch controls — so you can adjust volume, skip tracks, and take calls without reaching for your phone"

5. SPECIFICATIONS TABLE:
   Driver Size | Frequency Response | Bluetooth | Weight | Water Resistance | Charging

6. SOCIAL PROOF:
   "★★★★★ 'Best earbuds I've owned in this price range. The noise cancellation rivals my $300 pair.' — Verified Buyer"
   "[Join 15,000+ happy customers]"

7. URGENCY:
   "[Free 2-day shipping on orders over $50] [30-day money-back guarantee]"

8. CTA: "Add to Cart — Free Shipping & 30-Day Returns"

TONE: Confident, premium, specific. Not "amazing sound quality" (vague) — instead, describe WHAT makes it sound good and HOW the listener experiences it.

AVOID: "Game-changing," "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "best-in-class," "unparalleled" (empty superlatives). Every claim must be specific.

AMAZON BULLET POINTS (5):
Format: [BENEFIT IN CAPS] — [Supporting detail]
Each bullet: one benefit + one specific detail

SEO: Include "noise cancelling wireless earbuds" in headline and first sentence. Include "wireless earbuds for work calls" in feature descriptions. Include "long battery wireless earbuds" in battery description.
```

## Notes

- Product descriptions should help customers visualize owning and using the product
- Benefits always outperform features in conversion — lead with outcomes
- Specific numbers outperform vague claims ("24-hour battery" beats "all-day battery")
- Social proof is critical — include ratings, reviews, and trust signals wherever possible
- Always optimize for mobile — most product browsing happens on phones
