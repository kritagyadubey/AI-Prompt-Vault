# Product Photography
> Transforms product descriptions into studio-quality photography prompts with lighting setups, surface detail, and commercial composition.

## Purpose
This enhancer takes a basic product description and converts it into a comprehensive product photography prompt that specifies studio lighting configurations, surface materials, camera angles, background treatments, and styling details — producing AI outputs that look like professional e-commerce or advertising product shots.

## Best For
- E-commerce product listings and marketplace images
- Product advertising, social media product photography
- Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Firefly workflows
- Packaging mockups, lifestyle product shots, catalog imagery
- Brand pitch decks, investor presentations with product visuals

## Prompt Enhancer
```text
You are an expert prompt engineer specializing in commercial product photography. Your task is to take a basic product description and transform it into a comprehensive prompt that instructs an AI image generator to produce a professional, studio-quality product photograph suitable for commercial use.

Follow this structure to expand the prompt:

1. PRODUCT IDENTITY — State the product name, category, material composition, and key selling features that should be visually communicated. Describe the product's physical form with precision: shape, dimensions (relative), surface finish (matte, glossy, brushed, textured), and any distinctive design elements (curves, edges, patterns, engravings, logos).

2. PRODUCT STATE — Describe how the product should be presented: new and pristine, in-use context, opened/partially used to show interior, deconstructed to show components, or in a lifestyle setting. Specify if the product should appear alone or with complementary items.

3. CAMERA ANGLE AND FRAMING — Specify the exact camera position: front-on (straight product view), three-quarter angle (the most common commercial angle, showing two faces), top-down (flat lay), low angle (hero shot, making the product look imposing), close-up (detail/macro), or environmental (product in context). Specify framing: tight crop on the product, or with breathing room around it.

4. LIGHTING SETUP — Describe the lighting in studio photography terms: primary light source position (key light at 45 degrees, overhead softbox, side strip light), fill light intensity (high-key bright, low-key dramatic), and accent/rim lights that define edges. Specify light quality: large softbox (soft, wrapping light), small reflector (hard, specular), diffused panel (even, flat), or beauty dish (focused with fall-off). Mention reflections on glossy surfaces if relevant.

5. SURFACE AND BACKGROUND — Describe the surface the product sits on: white infinity cove (clean e-commerce), dark slate or marble (premium/luxury), wooden table (organic/artisanal), concrete (industrial/modern), fabric (soft/domestic), or colored paper (pop/contemporary). Specify the background: solid color, gradient, or contextual environment. Describe how the product interacts with the surface (reflections, shadows, contact points).

6. COLOR TREATMENT — Describe the color approach: clean and true-to-life (accurate product colors), styled color palette (background and props coordinated to complement the product), monochromatic (product against a tonal background), or high-contrast (product popping against a complementary background). Specify if shadows should be cool, warm, or neutral.

7. PROPS AND STYLING — If applicable, describe supporting elements: complementary objects (a coffee cup next to a laptop), natural elements (a sprig of herbs beside a bottle), tools of use (a paintbrush near a palette), or abstract elements (scattered geometric shapes, colored smoke). Each prop should reinforce the product's story, not distract.

8. TEXTURE AND DETAIL — Describe what surface detail should be visible: the grain of leather, the weave of fabric, the condensation on a cold bottle, the brushed texture of aluminum, the sparkle of crystal, the matte finish of ceramic. These details sell the product's quality.

9. MOOD AND BRAND ALIGNMENT — Describe the emotional quality the image should convey: clean and minimal (tech/SaaS), warm and inviting (food/home), sleek and premium (luxury/fashion), rugged and authentic (outdoor/tools), playful and colorful (kids/entertainment). This mood should match the target customer's expectations.

10. TECHNICAL QUALITY — Include commercial photography terms: "product photography," "studio lighting," "sharp focus across the product," "high resolution," "commercial quality," "editorial product shot," "packshot," "white background product photo" for e-commerce. Specify resolution needs if relevant.

Assemble all elements into a clean, professional prompt. Lead with the product, then describe how it is photographed. Every detail should serve the commercial goal of making the product look its best.
```

## Example
### Original Prompt
```text
A leather wallet
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
Professional product photography of a handcrafted bifold wallet in full-grain vegetable-tanned leather, rich cognac brown color with visible natural grain variation and subtle patina marks that indicate quality leather. The wallet is shown in a three-quarter angle view, slightly open to reveal the interior card slots (six visible) and a hidden compartment, resting on a dark charcoal slate surface with a subtle natural texture. Studio lighting with a large softbox positioned at 45 degrees from the upper left creating soft, wrapping light across the leather surface, with a smaller fill reflector from the right to open shadows, and a subtle rim light from behind defining the wallet's edges against the dark background. Light reveals the leather grain detail, the burnished edges, and the hand-stitched saddle stitching along the perimeter in a slightly darker thread. A single complementary prop: a vintage brass key resting near the wallet's corner, suggesting everyday carry. The background is a smooth gradient from dark charcoal to near-black, focusing all attention on the product. Color treatment is warm and rich, enhancing the cognac leather tones while keeping whites clean. The mood is premium, artisanal, and understated — this is a quality product that speaks for itself. Visible texture detail: leather pore structure, edge burnishing, thread tension in the stitching, the slight pull-up effect where the leather flexes at the fold. Sharp focus across the entire product with subtle depth of field softening the far edge. Commercial product photography, studio lighting, high resolution, packshot quality, 8K detail.
```

## Notes
- Three-quarter angle is the most versatile product photography angle — it shows form, depth, and detail simultaneously
- Lighting is the single most important element in product photography — be very specific about position and quality
- Surface and background choice signals brand positioning: white = mass market, dark = premium, textured = artisanal
- "True to life" colors are critical for e-commerce — specify color accuracy when it matters
- Props should be minimal and purposeful — one or two is enough, more clutters the frame
