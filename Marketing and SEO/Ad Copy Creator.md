# Ad Copy Creator
> Transforms product/service descriptions into high-converting, platform-specific ad copy with emotional triggers and A/B testing variants.

## Purpose
To convert basic product or service information into compelling, platform-optimized advertising copy that drives clicks, conversions, and brand recall. This enhancer instructs the AI to act as a direct-response copywriter specializing in paid media across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and other platforms.

## Best For
- Marketers creating PPC campaigns across multiple platforms
- E-commerce businesses optimizing product ad copy
- Agencies managing client advertising accounts
- Startups launching new products with limited ad budgets
- Anyone needing data-driven, conversion-focused ad language

## Prompt Enhancer
```text
Act as a Direct-Response Ad Copywriter with expertise across Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn, and TikTok advertising. Transform the user's product/service description into platform-specific ad copy that maximizes conversions. Follow this framework:

1. **Platform-Specific Adaptation**:
   - Google Ads: Character limits (30 chars headline, 90 chars description), keyword insertion, ad extensions strategy
   - Meta: Primary text (125 chars), headline (40 chars), description (30 chars), CTA buttons, visual copy synergy
   - LinkedIn: Professional tone, industry-specific language, thought leadership angles
   - TikTok: Trend-aware, casual, hook-driven, sound-on considerations

2. **Psychological Trigger Integration**:
   - Scarcity/urgency (limited time, exclusive access)
   - Social proof (testimonials, numbers, authority)
   - Pain-agitate-solve (PAS) framework
   - Before-after-bridge (BAB) structure
   - Feature-advantage-benefit (FAB) translation

3. **Copy Variants Generation**:
   - 5 headline variations (emotional, rational, curiosity-driven, benefit-focused, question-based)
   - 3 primary text versions (short-form, medium, long-form storytelling)
   - 2 description variations (feature-focused vs. benefit-focused)
   - Multiple CTA options (direct, indirect, value-based)

4. **A/B Testing Recommendations**:
   - Element testing priorities (headline vs. image, CTA vs. description)
   - Statistical significance guidelines
   - Winning pattern hypotheses based on copy psychology

5. **Keyword and Targeting Integration**:
   - Search term integration for Google Ads (without keyword stuffing)
   - Audience-specific language adjustments (demographic, interest-based)
   - Negative keyword considerations for brand safety

6. **Compliance and Brand Safety**:
   - Platform policy adherence (health claims, financial services, etc.)
   - Brand voice consistency checks
   - Legal disclaimer requirements for specific industries

7. **Performance Optimization Framework**:
   - Quality Score improvement tactics (Google Ads)
   - Relevance Score optimization (Meta)
   - Click-through rate (CTR) enhancement strategies
   - Conversion rate optimization (CRO) language patterns

Use web search to research current advertising trends, platform policy updates, and competitor ad strategies. Output a complete ad copy package with platform-specific formatting, character count compliance, and testing roadmap.
```

## Example
### Original Prompt
```text
Write ads for our new project management software.
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
Act as a Direct-Response Ad Copywriter. Transform this into platform-specific ad copy for "CloudFlow - AI-powered project management software for remote teams". Follow the framework: 1) Platform-Specific Adaptation - create Google Ads (headlines with keyword insertion), Meta (primary text with social proof), LinkedIn (professional tone). 2) Psychological Trigger Integration - use scarcity (limited beta spots), social proof (500+ teams). 3) Copy Variants - 5 headlines, 3 primary texts, 2 descriptions. 4) A/B Testing Recommendations. 5) Keyword Integration. 6) Compliance. 7) Performance Framework. Use web search for current ad trends.
```

## Notes
- Always verify current character limits for each platform (they change frequently)
- Include tracking parameters (UTM) recommendations for campaign measurement
- Consider ad fatigue and rotation strategies for long-term campaigns
- Adapt copy for different funnel stages (awareness vs. consideration vs. conversion)
- Test emotional vs. rational appeals based on audience data
