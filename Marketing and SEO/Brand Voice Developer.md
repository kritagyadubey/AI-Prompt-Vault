# Brand Voice Developer
> Transforms brand attributes into comprehensive voice guidelines with tone matrices, vocabulary rules, and application examples across channels.

## Purpose
To convert abstract brand personality traits into actionable voice guidelines with specific rules, examples, and adaptation strategies for different contexts. This enhancer instructs the AI to act as a brand strategist who develops consistent, distinctive brand communication.

## Best For
- Marketing teams establishing new brand identities
- Content creators needing consistent brand voice
- Agencies developing brand guidelines for clients
- Startups differentiating through brand personality
- Anyone needing to standardize brand communication across channels

## Prompt Enhancer
```text
Act as a Brand Voice Strategist with expertise in linguistic branding, tonal consistency, and cross-channel communication. Transform the user's brand attributes into comprehensive voice guidelines. Follow this framework:

1. **Brand Personality Foundation**:
   - Core personality traits (3-5 adjectives with explanations)
   - Brand archetypes alignment (if applicable)
   - Voice spectrum positioning (e.g., formal←→casual, serious←→playful, authoritative←→approachable)
   - Differentiation from competitor voices

2. **Tone Matrix Development**:
   - 5-7 primary tones with definitions (e.g., "confident but not arrogant")
   - Context-specific tone variations (social media, customer support, error messages, celebrations)
   - Emotional range boundaries (what the brand would never say)
   - Audience-specific adaptations (B2B vs. B2C, expert vs. novice)

3. **Vocabulary and Language Rules**:
   - Words to use (brand-approved terminology, power words)
   - Words to avoid (jargon, competitor language, outdated terms)
   - Sentence structure preferences (length, complexity, rhythm)
   - Punctuation and grammar rules (Oxford comma, exclamation marks, etc.)
   - Capitalization guidelines (title case, sentence case, all caps)

4. **Channel-Specific Adaptations**:
   - Website copy guidelines
   - Social media voice (platform-specific variations)
   - Email communication standards
   - Advertising and marketing materials
   - Customer service interactions
   - Internal communication guidelines

5. **Example Library Creation**:
   - Before/after rewrites for common brand messages
   - Industry-specific examples (product descriptions, about pages, CTAs)
   - Tone variations for the same message across channels
   - Bad examples (what NOT to do) with explanations

6. **Voice Governance System**:
   - Review checklist for brand voice compliance
   - Training guidelines for new team members
   - Escalation process for voice inconsistencies
   - Periodic voice audit recommendations

7. **Measurement and Evolution**:
   - Brand voice perception tracking methods
   - Feedback collection system design
   - Voice evolution roadmap (quarterly/annual reviews)
   - Competitive voice monitoring

Use web search to research current brand voice trends, successful brand voice case studies, and industry-specific language patterns. Output a comprehensive brand voice guide with practical examples and implementation rules.
```

## Example
### Original Prompt
```text
Our brand is friendly and professional. Help us develop a brand voice.
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
Act as a Brand Voice Strategist. Transform "friendly and professional" into comprehensive voice guidelines for "TechFlow - B2B SaaS for remote team collaboration". Follow the framework: 1) Brand Personality Foundation - define "approachable expertise" positioning. 2) Tone Matrix - develop 6 tones for different contexts. 3) Vocabulary Rules - tech terminology guidelines. 4) Channel Adaptations - professional LinkedIn vs. casual Twitter. 5) Example Library - 10 before/after rewrites. 6) Voice Governance. 7) Measurement. Use web search for B2B SaaS voice trends.
```

## Notes
- Always ground voice development in actual business goals and audience needs
- Include negative examples to clarify boundaries
- Consider cultural sensitivity and global adaptation requirements
- Document exceptions to voice rules (crisis communication, legal, etc.)
- Update voice guidelines as brand evolves and audience changes
