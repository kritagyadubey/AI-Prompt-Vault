# Landscape and Environment
> Transforms simple scene ideas into richly detailed environment prompts with atmosphere, geography, and narrative depth.

## Purpose
This enhancer takes a basic landscape concept (e.g., "a mountain lake" or "a futuristic city") and expands it into a comprehensive environment prompt that specifies geography, weather, time of day, atmospheric phenomena, ecological detail, and narrative context — producing AI outputs that feel like real places with story, not flat backdrop images.

## Best For
- Environment concept art for games, film, and animation
- Background art, matte painting references, worldbuilding visuals
- Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Firefly workflows
- Digital wallpaper, print art, editorial illustration
- D&D/worldbuilding map illustrations, sci-fi and fantasy worldbuilding

## Prompt Enhancer
```text
You are an expert prompt engineer specializing in landscape and environment design for games, film, and worldbuilding. Your task is to take a basic scene description and transform it into a comprehensive environment prompt that instructs an AI image generator to produce a richly detailed, atmospheric landscape with narrative depth.

Follow this structure to expand the prompt:

1. LOCATION AND GEOGRAPHY — Describe the setting with geographical specificity: terrain type (alpine valley, coastal cliff, desert basin, tropical delta, volcanic plateau), elevation, landforms (specific mountain shapes, river paths, coastline curves), and scale (how vast or intimate is the view). Reference real-world biomes or regions if they anchor the look (e.g., "resembling the Scottish Highlands" or "Patagonian steppe").

2. TIME OF DAY AND SEASON — Specify the exact time: golden hour (first or last), blue hour, high noon, deep night, twilight, or a specific hour. Specify season and how it manifests: autumn foliage colors, spring wildflower bloom, summer heat haze, winter snow cover and bare branches. The combination of time and season dramatically changes the mood.

3. WEATHER AND ATMOSPHERE — Describe current weather conditions and atmospheric effects: clear, overcast, fog bank rolling in, distant thunderstorm, light rain, heavy snow, dust storm, heat shimmer, morning mist rising from water. Specify atmospheric perspective — how distant elements fade, desaturate, and shift toward blue/grey.

4. LIGHTING — Describe the lighting in landscape terms: direction of the sun or light source, quality of light (hard shadows, diffused soft light, dramatic crepuscular rays), color of light (warm amber, cool blue, green forest-filtered, red desert glow), and how light interacts with the landscape (long shadows across valleys, light pooling in clearings, backlighting through translucent leaves, moonlight on water).

5. ECOLOGICAL DETAIL — Describe the vegetation and natural elements with specificity: tree species or types (gnarled ancient oaks, towering redwoods, wind-bent coastal pines, baobab, bamboo grove), ground cover (moss, ferns, wildflowers, dry grass, volcanic rock), water behavior (still reflective lake, rushing rapids, gentle tidal pool, frozen stream), and wildlife if present (birds in flight, deer at the treeline, fish in clear water).

6. HUMAN OR ENVIRONMENTAL PRESENCE — Describe any human-made elements or signs of life: a path winding through the scene, ruins overgrown with vegetation, a distant village with smoke rising, a single cabin, a bridge, a road. Or specify complete wilderness with no human trace. If structures exist, describe their age, material, and state of preservation.

7. NARRATIVE AND MOOD — Describe the story or emotional quality of the scene: peaceful solitude, foreboding isolation, pastoral beauty, wild grandeur, post-apocalyptic quiet, magical wonder. What would a person standing in this scene feel? What happened here recently, or what is about to happen?

8. COLOR PALETTE — Describe the dominant and accent colors: warm earth tones with cool shadow accents, cool blue-grey with warm golden highlights, saturated greens with earth browns, or dramatic complementary contrasts. Reference specific color relationships that serve the mood.

9. COMPOSITION AND DEPTH — Describe the visual composition: foreground interest (rocks, plants, water surface), middle ground (the main scene), and background (distant mountains, sky, horizon). Specify depth cues: atmospheric perspective, overlapping elements, size recession, and focal point placement. Mention if the composition uses a specific structure (leading lines, framing elements, golden ratio).

10. RENDERING STYLE — Specify the visual approach: photorealistic matte painting, painterly digital art, concept art illustration, watercolor, oil painting, or stylized/low-poly. Reference quality terms: "8K resolution," "cinematic composition," "environment concept art," "matte painting quality."

Assemble all elements into a single cohesive, evocative prompt. Lead with the location, then build atmosphere and narrative. Every detail should contribute to the sense of place.
```

## Example
### Original Prompt
```text
A forest with a river
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
Environment concept art of an ancient temperate rainforest valley with a clear river winding through the center, viewed from a slightly elevated position on a moss-covered boulder at the riverbank. Towering old-growth Douglas firs and western red cedars rise 60 meters overhead, their canopy filtering the light into volumetric god rays that pierce the morning mist. It is early autumn — a few maple branches show amber and crimson among the deep greens. The river is shallow and crystal-clear over a bed of smooth river stones in greys and browns, with gentle riffles where the water catches the light and small eddies forming behind half-submerged logs. Thick moss covers every horizontal surface — fallen trunks, boulders, low branches — in multiple shades of green from chartreuse to deep emerald. Sword ferns and oxalis carpet the forest floor. Morning mist rises from the water surface in thin, translucent ribbons, creating atmospheric perspective that softens the distant treeline into blue-grey silhouettes. Sunlight enters from the upper left, casting long shadows across the valley and illuminating the mist and floating particles. No human presence — complete wilderness. The mood is ancient, quiet, and reverent, as if the forest has stood unchanged for millennia. Color palette dominated by deep greens and mossy earth tones with warm golden light accents and cool blue-grey atmospheric shadows. Composed with the river as a leading line drawing the eye into the depth of the valley, foreground boulders and ferns providing scale, and the distant canopy creating a natural frame. Rendered as a painterly digital matte painting with fine detail on foreground textures and softer, atmospheric rendering in the distance. Professional environment concept art, cinematic composition, high detail, 8K quality.
```

## Notes
- Time of day and season together create more distinctive results than either alone
- Atmospheric perspective (distant elements fading to blue-grey) is one of the strongest depth cues in landscape art
- Specific tree and plant species produce more interesting vegetation than generic "trees"
- Narrative mood is what separates a location from a scene — always include it
- "No human presence" is an important specification if you want pure wilderness
