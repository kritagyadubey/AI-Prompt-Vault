# Character Designer
> Converts basic character concepts into detailed design specifications with anatomy, costume, silhouette, and expression detail.

## Purpose
This enhancer takes a character idea (e.g., "a warrior princess" or "a robot detective") and expands it into a comprehensive design prompt that covers anatomy, body language, costume construction, material details, accessories, color strategy, and expression — producing AI outputs that look like intentional character designs rather than generic illustrations.

## Best For
- Character concept art for games, animation, comics, and storytelling
- RPG character design, D&D character portraits, virtual avatars
- Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Leonardo AI workflows
- Costume design, character sheet references, character lineup sheets
- Book cover characters, editorial character illustrations

## Prompt Enhancer
```text
You are an expert prompt engineer specializing in character design for games, animation, and storytelling. Your task is to take a basic character description and transform it into a comprehensive design prompt that instructs an AI image generator to produce a professional, distinctive character concept.

Follow this structure to expand the prompt:

1. CHARACTER ESSENTIALS — State the character's name (if applicable), role/archetype, world/setting, and personality summary. Define age range, gender presentation, and the core visual hook — the single detail that makes this character instantly recognizable.

2. BODY AND ANATOMY — Describe the body type with specificity: height impression (tall, petite, stocky), build (athletic, lean, broad, wiry), proportions (realistic, stylized, chibi, exaggerated), and any distinctive physical features (scars, tattoos, birthmarks, prosthetics, unusual skin texture, non-human features). Specify posture and body language — how this character carries themselves.

3. FACE AND EXPRESSION — Describe facial structure: face shape, eye shape and color (with detail — "narrow dark eyes with amber flecks"), nose, mouth, jawline, and any distinctive features. Specify the default expression and emotional register — what does this character look like when they're at rest? What does their face communicate about their personality?

4. HAIR — Describe hair with construction detail: style (short crop, long flowing, braided, tied back, asymmetric), texture (straight, wavy, curly, coily), color (with highlights, roots, gradients if any), and how it moves or sits. Include facial hair if applicable. Hair is a critical silhouette element — be specific.

5. COSTUME AND CLOTHING — Describe the outfit as constructed garments, not vague categories: "fitted leather jacket with silver buckle closures over a loose linen tunic" not just "fantasy clothes." Specify layers, fit (tight, loose, draped), how garments interact (tucked, belted, layered), and the state of the clothing (pristine, weathered, patched, battle-worn).

6. MATERIALS AND TEXTURES — Describe fabric and material rendering: leather grain, metal sheen, cloth weave, fur texture, glass transparency, wood grain, rubber flexibility. Specify how materials respond to light — matte, glossy, iridescent, translucent, metallic.

7. ACCESSORIES AND PROPS — Describe any equipment, jewelry, weapons, tools, or标志性 items that define the character: "a brass telescope worn at the hip," "a neural interface cable running from behind the left ear," "a shield with a cracked surface and faded heraldry." Each accessory should tell a story about the character.

8. COLOR PALETTE — Define the character's color scheme: primary garment color, secondary color, accent color, and how color is distributed across the design. Explain color choices in relation to character role (e.g., "deep crimson for the commanding presence, black for authority, gold trim for rank"). Specify if the palette is limited or complex.

9. SILHOUETTE — Describe what the character looks like as a solid black shape — the most critical test of good character design. What elements create the distinctive outline? Head shape, hair volume, shoulder width, weapon/profile, cape, or unique geometry?

10. ART STYLE AND MEDIUM — Specify the visual style: realistic digital painting, anime/manga, Western comic, painterly concept art, flat vector, 3D render, pixel art, or watercolor illustration. Reference specific game, film, or artist styles if relevant. Specify if this is a turnaround sheet (multiple angles), a single portrait, or an action pose.

Assemble all elements into a single cohesive prompt. Lead with the character's identity, then build outward through physical, costume, and stylistic details. Every choice should serve character storytelling.
```

## Example
### Original Prompt
```text
A female elf ranger
```

### Enhanced Prompt
```text
Character concept art of Serelith, a 140-year-old wood elf ranger and tracker from an ancient forest realm, personality: patient, watchful, quietly fierce, with a dry humor that surfaces unexpectedly. Tall and lean with elongated limbs and slightly pointed ears that extend past the jawline, olive-brown skin with faint bioluminescent freckle patterns on the cheekbones and forearms — a trait of her bloodline. Narrow green-gold eyes with vertical slit pupils, sharp jawline, thin lips set in a neutral, observant expression. Silver-white hair in a single thick braid draped over the left shoulder, with loose strands framing the face, fine texture catching light like fiber optics. Wearing a fitted dark forest-green leather vest with embossed leaf patterns over a loose cream linen shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown trousers tucked into knee-high leather boots with braided lacing, and a hooded Cloak of woven spider-silk that shifts between grey and green depending on light. Materials rendered with detail: the leather vest shows grain and minor scratches from use, the linen has a visible loose weave, the cloak has an iridescent quality. Accessories: a short curved bow across the back with a quiver of fletched arrows (each fletching a different muted color from different bird species she has tracked), a bone-handled knife at the belt, and a small silver compass pendant. Color palette is grounded earth tones — forest green, bark brown, cream, with the silver-white hair and bioluminescent freckles as contrasting focal points. Silhouette is tall and narrow with the braid and bow creating a distinctive asymmetric outline. Rendered as a painterly digital concept art piece with visible brushwork on fabrics and environment, fine detail on the face, and atmospheric forest background with soft bokeh. Professional concept art quality, character design, high detail.
```

## Notes
- The silhouette test is the most important character design principle — if the character isn't recognizable as a black shape, the design isn't working
- "Show, don't tell" applies to character design: instead of saying "she is brave," describe the scar she earned and how she stands
- Material rendering detail separates professional concept art from generic illustration
- Always specify the expression — AI generators default to neutral/blank without guidance
- Color palette should connect to character story, not just look good
